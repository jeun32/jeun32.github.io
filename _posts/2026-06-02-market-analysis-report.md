## 한국 AI CapEx Rush? (메리츠증권, 2026.06.02)
This report, "Korean AI CapEx Rush?", discusses the potential for a significant AI infrastructure investment cycle in Korea, driven by a global economic shift towards an "AI Economy" (Token).

**Key Findings & Insights:**

*   **Historical Investment Cycle Pattern:** The report highlights a recurring pattern in major infrastructure cycles (e.g., railroads, electricity, internet):
    1.  **Platform Companies:** First to rally, capturing end-user demand.
    2.  **Infrastructure Providers:** Follow, as demand creates bottlenecks in infrastructure.
    3.  **Manufacturing Companies:** Last and often longest-lasting rally, as they provide the necessary capacity and components for infrastructure.
*   **AI Cycle Alignment:** The current AI boom fits this pattern. Initial rallies were seen in AI platform providers (e.g., NVIDIA, CSPs), followed by infrastructure pure-plays, and now strongly in manufacturing companies addressing AI infrastructure bottlenecks (memory, networking, power).
*   **Jensen Huang's Visit to Korea:** The upcoming visit by NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang is seen as a pivotal event. While superficially interpreted as a focus on "physical AI" or "robotics," the report argues for a broader interpretation: the potential for **"Korean-style AI infrastructure ecosystem creation."**
*   **Shift from US-centric to Korea-centric AI CapEx:** Historically, AI infrastructure investment has been US-centric. The recent stock movements in Korean SI firms and internet companies (like Naver) suggest market anticipation that this AI infrastructure pattern could begin in Korea.
*   **Broad Impact on Korean Industry:** The market reaction to Jensen Huang's potential visit wasn't just about who *creates* AI services, but rather **who will build, operate, and form the industrial ecosystem around the physical AI infrastructure in Korea.** This includes GPU clusters, AI data centers, power/cooling, cloud operations, telecom networks, manufacturing data, robotics, and DX solutions.
*   **Sovereign AI as a Key Driver:** The concept of "Sovereign AI" is crucial. Nations are increasingly seeking to build their own AI infrastructure, utilize domestic data, and develop AI models tailored to their industries. Korea, with its strong manufacturing and digital industries (semiconductors, batteries, automotive, telecom), is well-positioned for an "AI factory" concept.
*   **Investment Implications for Korea:**
    *   If a "Korean AI data center construction and operation" theme emerges, initial beneficiaries would be **large enterprise SI companies, internet/platform companies, and telecom operators** with cloud operation experience.
    *   Key factors to watch for these companies include solutions for **power supply, GPU procurement, and securing anchor tenants.**
    *   While the initial rally might be in platform/infrastructure companies, the report emphasizes that this ultimately benefits **manufacturing companies**, which are currently leading the global AI rally.

In essence, the report suggests that Korea is on the cusp of a significant AI infrastructure investment wave, potentially mirroring past economic transformations. Jensen Huang's visit could be a catalyst for this, shifting the focus from abstract AI potential to concrete plans for building and operating a comprehensive AI ecosystem within Korea, with broad implications for various industries.
